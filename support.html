Common questions

  • Does the app need internet? No. Lessons, vocabulary, and audio work offline. Ads need an internet connection.
  • Do I need an account? No. Your progress is stored on your device.
  • Speaking practice is not working. Check that microphone access is allowed in your device settings, and that speech recognition is available. You can always type your answer instead.
  • I want study reminders. Turn them on in the app settings. They are off by default.
  • How do I delete my data? Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.
  • Ads. Ads are managed by Google AdMob. See our privacy policy for details.
