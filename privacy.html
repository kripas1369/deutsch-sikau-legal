Privacy Policy — Deutsch Sikau

Deutsch Sikau (the “App”) is a German learning app for A1–B2 level learners, with vocabulary, grammar courses, listening, speaking and writing practice, and mock tests. The App is developed by Kripas Khatiwada (“we”, “us”).

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Information stored on your device

Your study progress, settings, spaced-repetition schedule, and saved answers are stored only on your device. We do not run a user account system and we do not operate a server that collects your learning data. Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including banner ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads, and optional rewarded video ads. Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one to unlock certain content for a limited time.

To deliver and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process device information, IP address, and advertising identifiers, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We do not sell your personal data.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we show a consent form before personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in the App settings.
  • iOS: the App asks for permission to track your activity across apps (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised.

3. Microphone and speech

The speaking practice feature uses your microphone only after you tap the microphone button. Your voice is converted to text to check your pronunciation. We do not record, store, or upload your voice. Where your device supports on-device speech recognition, it is used; otherwise the speech service built into your phone’s operating system (Apple or Google) may process the audio under its own privacy policy.

Pronunciation audio in the App is played from files stored in the App and does not require an internet connection.

4. Notifications

Study reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are off until you turn them on. We do not send push notifications from a server.

5. Analytics and tracking

The App does not include first-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or account sign-in.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

  • Turn study reminders on or off in the App settings.
  • Deny microphone access in your device settings; speaking practice will then use typed answers.
  • Change ad consent or tracking permission in the App settings or your device settings.
  • Delete all App data by uninstalling the App.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or another region with data-protection rights, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete any personal information we hold. We hold very little because most data stays on your device.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
